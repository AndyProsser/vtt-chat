# Groups Panel User Guide

The **Groups** panel (left side of the screen) shows all players in your campaign and which voice group they're currently in during an active session.

## Overview

- **Groups** are voice channels where players connect and speak
- Each player avatar appears under their current group
- Avatars show **speaking indicators** (glow animation) when a player is actively speaking
- Avatars show **mute badges** when a player (or the DM) has silenced them
- Groups can be created, organized, and deleted by the DM during a session

## Understanding Player Status

### Speaking Indicator (Glow Animation)

When a player is actively speaking:

- Their avatar glows with a subtle pulse animation
- This happens in real-time as they speak
- If you see a glow, that player is currently talking (and not muted)

### Mute Badge

A small mute icon appears on a player's avatar when:

- **They muted themselves** — via the mute button in their own audio controls
- **The DM silenced them** — for dramatic effect, off-the-record moments, or mechanical effects (e.g., silenced spell)

When muted:

- Other players **cannot hear** their voice
- The DM and spectators (if enabled) **can still hear** them
- Their avatar **does not glow** even if they try to speak

## Accessing Your Mute Button

### Mute Yourself

1. Look for the **Audio Panel** (usually bottom-right or in your character sheet)
2. Find the **microphone icon** with a slash through it (mute button)
3. Click to mute; click again to unmute
4. Your mute status appears instantly on your avatar in the Groups panel

### What Others See

When you mute:

- Your avatar immediately shows a mute badge
- Your avatar stops glowing (even if you're speaking)
- Other players see you're muted without needing to ask

## DM Muting (Creative Effects)

The DM can apply mute effects:

- **Silence Spell** — Your character is magically silenced; you're muted for everyone except the DM
- **Underwater Scenes** — Muffled audio to simulate depth
- **Off-the-Record Whisper** — Quick private moment; you're muted from the main group

You'll see a mute badge on your avatar when the DM applies these effects. **You can still speak** (you hear yourself), but others in that group don't hear you until the effect ends.

## Tips for Better Audio

- **Mute yourself during crowd noise** if you're not speaking
- **Position your avatar** in a group with people you're conversing with
- **Watch speaking glows** to know when someone is taking their turn
- **Listen for the DM** to notice when they move you to a private moment (whisper group)

## Groups Hierarchy During Sessions

Groups are organized by type:

- **MAIN** — The primary group where the action happens; spectators hear this group
- **GROUP** (named) — Additional voice channels the DM creates for side conversations, scouts, or parallel scenes
- **PRIVATE** (Whisper) — Secret off-the-record moment; no one outside this group can hear or see content

## Accessibility

The Groups panel respects your system accessibility settings:

- **Reduced Motion:** If you have "Reduce Motion" enabled in system settings, speaking glows fade smoothly instead of pulsing
- **Screen Readers:** Avatar names and status (muted/speaking) are described for assistive technology
- **Keyboard Navigation:** You can navigate between groups and players using arrow keys and Enter

## Troubleshooting

**I don't see the Groups panel:**

- It appears on the left side during an active session
- If hidden, look for a collapse icon (☰) and expand it

**My speaking indicator isn't glowing:**

- Verify you're not muted (check your Audio Panel)
- Check that your microphone is active in your browser settings
- Other players should see your glow if you're speaking unmuted

**My avatar disappeared:**

- You may have been moved to a private group (Whisper)
- Or the session ended; groups are cleared between sessions
- Check with the DM if you're unsure

**I'm muted but I didn't click mute:**

- The DM may have applied a mute effect for gameplay reasons
- Wait for them to un-mute you, or ask in text chat
